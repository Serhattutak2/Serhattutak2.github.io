İlk Tapu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Information we collect

İlk Tapu requires no account and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your own device and are not sent to us.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. İlk Tapu shows only non-personalized ads and does not request Apple's App Tracking (ATT) permission. AdMob may process information such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (from your IP address), device model and ad interactions in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
